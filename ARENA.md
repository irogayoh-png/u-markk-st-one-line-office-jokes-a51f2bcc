# ARENA — One-Line Office Jokes (u-markk-st-one-line-office-jokes-a51f2bcc)

Juri: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, pairwise dua arah, kriteria berurutan:
1. Satu baris lelucon tentang kehidupan kantor, bukan cerita, slogan, atau daftar; kalah lawan lelucon kantor mana pun yang mendarat tawanya.
2. Berputar pada detail nyata kehidupan kantor — rapat, email, printer, mesin kopi — bukan omongan generik soal kerja.
3. Tawa mendarat di kata-kata terakhir, sehingga belokan tiba di ketukan terakhir.
4. Jelas dalam sekali baca, tanpa setup yang butuh penjelasan.
5. Memakai kata-kata segar, bukan lelucon yang sudah beredar di internet.
Gate: UTF-8, 1–199 karakter, bukan spasi saja, satu baris. Bila kualitas setara, pilih yang LEBIH PENDEK.

## 16 kandidat (semua lelucon kantor baru, suara orang pertama)

- C1 (118): The office printer only jams when the document is urgent, confidential, and already five minutes late for the meeting.
- C2 (103): Someone replied-all 'thanks!' to a 200-person email, and 47 people replied-all 'thanks!' to the thanks.
- C3 (126): The coffee machine broke on Monday, so by Tuesday we were holding a meeting about holding a meeting to fix the coffee machine.
- C4 (126): Monday's meeting started with 'this will be quick,' which is office for 'cancel your lunch and say goodbye to your afternoon.'
- C5 (117): Our daily stand-up is fifteen minutes of sitting down to explain why yesterday's fifteen minutes didn't fix anything.
- C6 (98): My calendar is so full that I had to book a meeting with myself, and I still showed up late to it.
- C7 (117): IT made me change my password again, so now it's the same password as before, but with the shame of a capital letter.
- C8 (117): I set my out-of-office reply to 'I'm away,' and my inbox replied by forwarding me to twelve people who are also away.
- C9 (119): The printer printed my confidential salary review, then jammed, so now the whole floor knows my raise and my paper jam.
- C10 (121): The office coffee tastes like the printer ink, which explains why both of them only work when nobody actually needs them.
- C11 (135): Facilities set the thermostat to 'Arctic' so we'd stop fighting over it, and now we hold meetings in coats to discuss the heating bill.
- C12 (138): My badge stopped working on the one morning I was on time, so security watched me prove I work here to a door I've opened for three years.
- C13 (145): I spent three hours formatting a spreadsheet nobody will read, then presented it in a meeting nobody wanted, to people who asked for the summary.
- C14 (120): The printer jammed on page 47 of 48, so I reprinted all 48 pages and watched it jam on page 47 again, right on schedule.
- C15 (150): I replied-all 'unsubscribe' to the office newsletter, and now I get a weekly email confirming I am still subscribed to the emails about unsubscribing.
- C16 (137): HR's wellness email told us to take a break, so I took one and came back to 214 emails, a meeting invite, and a reminder to take a break.
